Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory · 2025–2026

Neural network surrogates inside a large-scale optimizer

Transient stability constraints are too expensive to enforce directly in AC optimal power flow, because each check needs a full dynamic simulation.

I trained a convolutional neural network to predict generator stability and embedded it, with its first and second derivatives, inside LLNL's nonlinear optimization solver. I then validated the surrogate-constrained solutions against full simulation.

UC Merced with LLNL · 2026

Calibrated probability of failure with conformal prediction

A single stability prediction isn't enough when a fault can occur at any bus. Operators need the probability that the system becomes unstable, and how sure that number is.

I led a project that learns the full distribution of the stability index over all fault locations and adds conformal prediction, which gives calibrated, distribution-free bounds on the probability of failure.
